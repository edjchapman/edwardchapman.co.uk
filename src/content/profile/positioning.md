I'm a senior software and platform engineer in London with nine-plus years
building production systems: backend and platform depth in Python and
Django, infrastructure ownership on AWS with Terraform, and product
engineering in React and TypeScript.

I own systems end to end — specification, architecture, infrastructure,
deploy pipeline, and the measurements that prove behaviour — and I design
for the moments things go wrong: crash boundaries, duplicate deliveries,
partial failures under load. The projects on this site demonstrate both,
live, with their numbers measured rather than asserted.

I've shipped across proptech (commercial real-estate financial modelling),
supply-chain and logistics, and energy platforms, most recently in senior and
lead roles. My current focus is AI-native engineering treated as engineering:
retrieval-grounded systems with citations, guardrails at trust boundaries,
and evaluation harnesses that run in CI — measured behaviour, not vibes.
