I'm a senior software and platform engineer based in London, with more than
nine years of experience building production systems. My core strengths are
backend engineering with Python and Django, cloud infrastructure on AWS and
Terraform, and product development with React and TypeScript.

I take ownership of systems from initial specification through architecture,
infrastructure, deployment, and observability. I design for the points where
production systems usually fail: crash boundaries, duplicate deliveries,
partial failures, and unpredictable behaviour under load. The projects on
this site show that approach in practice, supported by live demos and
measured results.

I've worked across proptech, supply chain and logistics, and energy
platforms, most recently in senior and lead roles. My current focus is
AI-native engineering approached with the same discipline as any other
production system: retrieval grounded in verifiable sources, guardrails at
trust boundaries, and evaluation harnesses that run continuously in CI.
