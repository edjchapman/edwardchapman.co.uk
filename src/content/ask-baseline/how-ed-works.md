Ed treats AI as a power tool in a harness: agentic tooling does the mechanical work, while architecture, judgement, and review stay human.[[how-i-work#body]] He works spec-first, holds evidence over claims — reliability numbers from load tests, AI quality from evaluation harnesses in CI that gate merges — and runs boundaries like content policy, commit standards, and quality gates as code on every change, so a violation is a red build, not a retrospective.[[how-i-work#body]]
